How to practise

  1. Choose a word from the list.
  2. Press Listen and pay attention to the stress and rhythm.
  3. Press Record yourself. Your browser may ask for permission to use the microphone.
  4. Listen to your recording and compare it with the model.
  5. Repeat the word until you feel comfortable saying it.
  6. Use Pronunciation Challenge when you are ready to test yourself.

Tip: Don't worry about sounding exactly like an American speaker. Focus first on the stressed syllable, the main vowel sounds, and saying the whole word clearly.
